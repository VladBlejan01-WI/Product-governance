## Governance notes

Record significant product decisions that affect scope, governance, architecture, service model, risk, or commercial arrangements. Link to ADRs when the decision has architectural impact.

## Maintenance guidance

- Keep records concise and evidence-based.
- Do not include confidential evidence in Git.
- Cross-reference supporting analysis and decisions using approved evidence locations.
