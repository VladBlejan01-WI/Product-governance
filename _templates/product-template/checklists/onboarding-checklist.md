# Onboarding checklist

Use this checklist to confirm a product is ready to enter common governance and support processes.

- [ ] Product metadata reviewed and valid.
- [ ] Product owner assigned and recorded.
- [ ] Technical and service ownership confirmed.
- [ ] Security and data classification reviewed.
- [ ] Licensing and commercial documentation checked.
- [ ] Dependencies and support arrangements reviewed.
- [ ] Portfolio catalog and risk register updated.
- [ ] Documentation links reviewed.
- [ ] Review date scheduled.
