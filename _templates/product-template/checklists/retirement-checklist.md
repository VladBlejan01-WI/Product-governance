# Retirement checklist

Use this checklist to govern product decommissioning or replacement.

- [ ] Retirement trigger confirmed.
- [ ] Business and technical owners informed.
- [ ] Data and evidence retention obligations reviewed.
- [ ] Licensing and contract closure steps identified.
- [ ] Support and service continuity handoff planned.
- [ ] User communication plan approved.
- [ ] Repository and portfolio updates completed.
- [ ] Final archive or evidence references captured.
