## Product summary

ZEISS INSPECT is reported as software for 3D inspection, dimensional analysis, reporting, and evaluation of optical and CT measurement data. ZEISS INSPECT Pro is the licensed edition reported to provide advanced professional inspection workflows, automation, custom reporting, and specialized analysis.

## Scope

The dossier records the two editions, the reported metrology/quality use, local desktop architecture and dependencies, reported PLM/CAD workflows, licensing and support boundaries, known operational issues, and governance evidence gaps. It does not establish the exact installation inventory, current entitlements, approved workstation configuration, quality-system validation, or deployment approval.
