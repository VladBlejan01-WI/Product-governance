The stakeholder intake reports local desktop deployment, ZEISS Quality Suite/driver dependencies, vendor installation packages and service packs, and recurring compatibility issues after Windows updates. WIRO ZEISS INSPECT Pro 2025 in December 2025 is reported; versions at other sites are TBD.

The requester supplied ZEISS's [System Requirements for ZEISS INSPECT Optical 3D](https://www.zeiss.com/metrology/en/software/zeiss-inspect/zeiss-inspect-optical-3d/system-requirements.html). The page was not retrievable for independent review in this session, and no minimum/recommended CPU, GPU, RAM, storage, OS, driver, or peripheral values are asserted here. The attached image was only a 20 × 20 px generic icon and did not contain readable computer specifications.
