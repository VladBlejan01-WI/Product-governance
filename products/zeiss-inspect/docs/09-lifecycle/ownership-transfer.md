# Ownership Transfer and Access

**Status:** Transfer process and required permissions not confirmed.

The stakeholder intake says the required systems, portals, distribution lists, repositories, and administrative permissions for the Product Owner role have not yet been assessed. It also reports no formal documented ownership-transfer process and identifies required IT administrator rights as a minimum handover item.

## Proposed handover checklist (not an approved workflow)

- Name accountable business, IT product, technical, Quality/Metrology, site, procurement, security, data, and support owners.
- Confirm the approved access required for ZEISS Quality Suite/license administration, vendor support, package repositories, local software deployment, PLM/CAD process material, and controlled contract records.
- Transfer access through approved identity and access-management processes; do not share credentials or copy them into Git.
- Confirm license/contract administration, renewal calendar, site/version inventory, support escalation, open risks, process validation, and pending approvals.
- Record formal ownership acceptance and required governance/board approvals in the approved internal system.

Actual access rights, identities, approvals, and formal transfer date remain TBD. This checklist does not grant permissions.
