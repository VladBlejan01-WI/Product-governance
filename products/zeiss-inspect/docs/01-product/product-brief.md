## Purpose

ZEISS INSPECT is reported to support 3D inspection, dimensional analysis, reporting, and evaluation of optical and computed-tomography (CT) measurement data. It is used in metrology, quality, product development, and production contexts.

## Editions

- **ZEISS INSPECT Pro:** licensed edition; intake reports advanced professional workflows, automation, custom reporting, and specialized analyses. Verify purchased entitlement and enabled modules.
