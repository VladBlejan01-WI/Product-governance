# Data, PLM Storage, and Recovery

## Reported data workflows

Stakeholder intake reports that ZEISS INSPECT projects are being standardized for storage in PLM and that measurement-data handling process documents exist. The exact PLM tenant/system, project locations, data formats, record ownership, retention, archival, backup, access controls, and recovery procedure were not supplied.

## Required controls

- Link the approved PLM storage work instruction and measurement-data strategy once their controlled locations are confirmed.
- Identify the data owner and applicable Quality/Metrology retention and traceability requirements.
- Confirm that inspection projects, reports, inputs, and required measurement evidence are backed up and recoverable under the applicable controlled process.
- Assess whether report/project content includes confidential product designs or personal data; define classification and access accordingly.
- Test recovery and CAD/PMI/MBD data traceability without copying production data into Git.

The repository must contain only stable references, not measurement projects, reports, customer/product test data, or named-user inventories.
