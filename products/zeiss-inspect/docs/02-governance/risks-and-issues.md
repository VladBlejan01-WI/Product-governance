# Risks and Issues

| ID | Candidate risk / intake evidence | Potential effect | Proposed treatment | Owner / rating |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Site software versions differ; WIRO Pro 2025 in December 2025 is reported | Workflow/reproducibility differences and support burden | Reconcile versions and test a coordinated baseline across sites | TBD |
| R2 | Windows-update compatibility issues and ZEISS Quality Suite driver dependencies are reported | Device or measurement workflow failure after endpoint updates | Identify supported Windows/driver combinations; pilot updates and document rollback | TBD |

| R7 | No formal security/risk assessment has been identified in intake | Security/privacy controls may be unknown | Assess actual endpoint, dongle/license mechanisms, drivers, data, access, and updates | TBD |
