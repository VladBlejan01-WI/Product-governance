# Lifecycle Status

**Current status:** Draft onboarding baseline. Stakeholder intake reports use at four metrology organizations/sites—Detmold, TWG, WISC, and WIRO—but the exact installed base and approved lifecycle status are unverified.

## Reported version event

Intake reports that WIRO upgraded to ZEISS INSPECT Pro 2025 in December 2025. Treat this as a stakeholder report until confirmed against endpoint, ZEISS Quality Suite, and site records. Other sites' versions and support status are TBD.

## Reported initiatives

- Standardize storage of ZEISS INSPECT projects in PLM.
- Increase use of PMI/MBD and STEP AP242 workflows.
- Harmonize software versions across sites.

## Lifecycle gates

1. **Identify:** verify editions, installed versions, sites, measurement systems, owners, and license allocation.
2. **Validate:** obtain current ZEISS system requirements, release/support information, entitlement, workstation/driver compatibility, and quality-process validation evidence.
3. **Standardize:** approve PLM storage, data classification, measurement methods, version baseline, and supported CAD/PMI workflows.
4. **Change:** define upgrade/service-pack testing, approval, rollback, and site coordination.
5. **Operate:** document support, backup/recovery, access, renewal, vulnerability, and periodic review responsibilities.

No approved deployment state, vendor support lifecycle, or version-harmonization decision is asserted by this draft.
