## Purpose

Provide engineering and production users with a dedicated system to measure and analyze inductive power components, including chokes and related components. The documented use cases include development/research, quality control, and production testing.

## Product boundary

The product record covers ed-k DPG10 PC software and its dedicated DPG measurement hardware. The manuals also describe the DPG20 family, optional 3-Phase Extension Unit (EXT1/EXT2/EXT3), and optional InterfaceBox INT1. Whether these optional devices or integrations are deployed locally is TBD.

## Users and locations

Engineering, laboratory, quality, and production testing are documented usage contexts. Actual Weidmüller user groups, process owner, location, deployment count, and production scope require internal confirmation.

## Success measures

Proposed measures, subject to owner agreement: validated software/hardware baseline; controlled and recoverable measurement records; approved endpoint and interface configuration; supported-user coverage; documented safety and support ownership; and entitlement/renewal readiness.
