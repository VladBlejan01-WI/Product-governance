# Exit Strategy

**Document status:** Draft baseline  
**Owner:** TBD  
**Review frequency:** Quarterly or on material change  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-24

> Plan for product replacement or retirement.

## Exit requirements

Export measurement data and reports; preserve required formats/readability; identify replacement; terminate licenses/support; remove installations/keys; archive evidence; communicate; dispose data; close integrations and budget.

## Risk

Proprietary formats and specialist workflows may affect migration. Validate export capability and retention needs before termination.
