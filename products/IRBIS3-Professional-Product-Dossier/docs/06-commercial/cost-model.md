**Document status:** Draft baseline  
**Owner:** TBD  
**Review frequency:** Quarterly or on material change

> Calculate TCO without inventing amounts.

## TCO structure

License/subscription or maintenance; optional modules; vendor support; reseller cost; packaging/deployment; workstation hardware; storage/backup; internal support; training; integrations; upgrade effort; retirement/export.

## Formula

Annual TCO = recurring vendor cost + allocated infrastructure + support effort + annualized change/training + risk contingency.

## Evidence

Each value needs currency, tax treatment, period, owner, source document, fixed/variable flag, and confidence.
