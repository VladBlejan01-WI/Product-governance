# Business Continuity

**Document status:** Draft baseline  
**Owner:** TBD  
**Review frequency:** Quarterly or on material change  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-24

> Plan for disruption.

## Scenarios

Workstation loss; license mechanism unavailable; corrupted measurement file; incompatible OS upgrade; vendor support delay; unavailable camera.

## Continuity options

Approved spare workstation/package, safeguarded entitlement evidence, backed-up data, export capability, documented vendor contact, and prioritized recovery. Validate feasibility.
