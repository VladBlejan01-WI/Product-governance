# Risks and Issues

**Document status:** Draft baseline  
**Owner:** TBD  
**Review frequency:** Quarterly or on material change  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-24

> Maintain product risks, not employee assessments.

## Initial risks

| ID | Risk | Impact | Response | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Entitlement or quantity unknown | Compliance/cost | Reconcile evidence and installs | Procurement |
| R2 | Unsupported version/OS | Security/continuity | Establish supported baseline | Technical Owner |
| R3 | Measurement data stored only locally | Data loss | Define approved storage/backup | Data Owner |
| R4 | Vendor-format dependency | Portability | Define export and exit approach | PO |
| R5 | Specialist knowledge concentration | Continuity | Training and runbooks | Business Owner |

## Issue rule

Issues must include owner, due date, status, evidence link, and escalation path.
