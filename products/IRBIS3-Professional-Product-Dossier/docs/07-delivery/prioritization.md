# Prioritization

**Document status:** Draft baseline  
**Owner:** TBD  
**Review frequency:** Quarterly or on material change  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-24

> Prioritize product work transparently.

## Method

Use risk reduction, compliance urgency, business impact, number of affected workflows, effort, dependency, and time criticality.

## Rule

Safety, legal, security, license, and data-loss risks receive explicit review before feature convenience.
