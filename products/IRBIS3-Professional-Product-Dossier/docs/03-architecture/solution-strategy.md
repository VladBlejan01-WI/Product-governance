> Record high-level solution direction.

## Strategy

Use vendor-supported desktop deployment with centrally governed entitlement, version baseline, package/configuration, documented storage, controlled export, and optional integrations only after architecture/security review.

## Trade-offs

Specialist functionality and vendor support are balanced against proprietary formats, workstation dependency, and commercial licensing.
