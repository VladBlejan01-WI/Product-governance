> Define value, outcomes, and strategic boundaries.

## Vision

A governed and supportable thermography analysis capability that lets authorized engineering users turn infrared measurement data into reliable evaluations and reports.

## Strategic themes

1. License and installation transparency
2. Supported and secure operation
3. Repeatable analysis and reporting
4. User capability and adoption
5. Controlled lifecycle and vendor dependency

## Assumptions

The product is vendor software, used primarily on Windows workstations, and receives measurement files from compatible thermal-imaging equipment. Confirm environment-specific details.
