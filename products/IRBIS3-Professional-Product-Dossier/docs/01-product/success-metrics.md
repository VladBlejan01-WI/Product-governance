> Define product-level measures without fabricated targets.

## Metrics

- Percentage of installations reconciled to entitlement
- Percentage on supported version
- Open critical vulnerabilities
- Support cases by category
- Time to fulfill approved access
- Users with current training
- Renewal readiness status
- Documentation health

## Target-setting rule

Targets require agreement with Business Owner, IT Support, Security, Procurement, and users.
