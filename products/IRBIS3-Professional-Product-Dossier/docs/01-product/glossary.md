## Terms

**Thermogram:** thermal image containing measurement information.

**Emissivity:** material property used in temperature evaluation.

**IRB:** InfraTec-related thermographic data format referenced by vendor material.

**Entitlement:** documented right to install or use software.

**SDK:** software development kit for integration or automation.
