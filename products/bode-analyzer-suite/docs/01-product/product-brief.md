# Product Brief

**Document status:** Draft baseline  
**Owner:** TBD  
**Review frequency:** Quarterly or on material change  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-10-15

## Purpose

Support electronics engineering and laboratory work by configuring measurements, acquiring and visualizing frequency-response data, and analyzing electronic circuits and control loops with compatible Bode measurement hardware.

## In scope

Software lifecycle, supported endpoint baseline, hardware and driver dependency, data handling, licensing, vendor support, user enablement, and governance evidence.

## Out of scope

Interpretation or approval of engineering results, unvalidated automation, shared-server design, and hardware asset ownership unless explicitly assigned.

## Users and locations

Intended users are engineering, electronics development, laboratory, verification, and test personnel with a justified need. Detmold is the initial relevant location in the supplied research. Actual users, installed base, additional locations, business owner, and key user are TBD.

## Product boundary

Bode Analyzer Suite is the software product. Bode 100 and Bode 500 are dependent measurement hardware, not separate software records in this dossier.
