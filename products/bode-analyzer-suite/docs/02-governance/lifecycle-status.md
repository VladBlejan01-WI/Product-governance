# Lifecycle Status

**Current status:** Onboarding baseline.

The supplied manuals describe BAS 3.52 and Bode 500 compatibility with BAS 3.50 or newer. They do not verify the installed BAS version, current vendor-supported release, hardware firmware, organizational entitlement, package status, or approval state.

## Required lifecycle gates

1. **Identify:** confirm software release, hardware model/firmware, location, owners, users, and use case.
2. **Validate:** verify vendor release documentation, licensing, installer provenance, supported OS, endpoint behavior, and representative measurement.
3. **Package:** document managed install, detection, update, uninstall, logging, rollback, and support process.
4. **Approve:** complete security/policy, criticality, data, commercial, and operational ownership decisions.
5. **Operate:** deploy to approved users, monitor version/vulnerability information, and perform periodic reviews.
