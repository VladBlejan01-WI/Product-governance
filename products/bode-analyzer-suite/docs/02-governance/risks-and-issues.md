# Risks and Issues

**Document status:** Draft baseline. Initial ratings are proposed only and require owner review.

| ID | Risk | Potential effect | Initial view | Treatment | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Unsupported Windows, driver, BAS, or firmware combination | Instrument unavailable or unreliable after change | Medium | Establish and test a versioned compatibility baseline before rollout | TBD |
| R2 | Hardware, cable, fixture, or calibration dependency | Software operates but measurements are invalid or unavailable | Medium | Maintain asset/calibration records and complete representative verification tests | TBD |
| R3 | Uncontrolled software update | Changed measurement behaviour, automation, or compatibility | Medium | Review release notes, test, manage updates, and retain rollback approach | TBD |
| R4 | Local-only storage of results or scripts | Loss of engineering evidence | Medium | Use approved corporate storage and define retention/backup | TBD |
| R5 | Unknown network, telemetry, activation, or updater behaviour | Security or privacy exposure | Medium until assessed | Test network behaviour and document necessary endpoints and controls | TBD |
| R6 | License/entitlement ambiguity | Compliance or operational interruption | Medium | Reconcile EULA, purchase entitlement, metric, and support terms | TBD |
| R7 | Measurement-method, fixture, or calibration error | Incorrect engineering conclusion | Potentially high | Training, controlled methods, calibration, and peer review | TBD |
| R8 | Single specialist or workstation | Delay during absence or endpoint failure | Low-medium | Name a backup key user and assess need for a validated spare workstation | TBD |
