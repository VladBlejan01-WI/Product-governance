## Supplied-source handling

The onboarding used supplied product-description and research-report documents, a quick-start guide, a user manual, and a Bode 500 technical-data sheet. Their contents have been summarized into this governance record. The documents themselves are not committed.

A supplied delivery note is procurement/asset evidence. Do not add it, copied identifiers, prices, contacts, addresses, or account details to Git. Store it in approved controlled records and add only a stable approved reference when one is available.
