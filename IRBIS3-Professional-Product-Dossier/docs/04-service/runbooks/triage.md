# Runbook: Support Triage

**Document status:** Draft baseline  
**Owner:** TBD  
**Review frequency:** Quarterly or on material change  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-24

> Guide repeatable troubleshooting.

## Checks

Confirm scope and impact; capture version/modules; verify file access; test with approved sample; check workstation changes; reproduce; separate data-specific from application-wide failure; consult known issues; sanitize evidence before vendor transfer.

## Escalation

Include contract/support ID, product version, module, reproducible steps, expected/actual, logs, environment, and approved sample.
