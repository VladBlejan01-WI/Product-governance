> One-page product definition.

## Purpose

Enable engineering users to analyze thermographic images and sequences and produce measurement evaluations and reports.

## In scope

Desktop software ownership, installation governance, entitlements, vendor relationship, user enablement, support, lifecycle, and documentation.

## Out of scope

Infrared camera hardware ownership unless assigned to this product; interpretation of measurement results; unapproved custom integrations.

## Users

Thermography specialists, test and validation engineers, R&D users, quality engineers, and trained support personnel. Validate the actual user groups internally.

## Success measures

Proposed: entitled installations, supported-version coverage, license compliance, successful report generation, support resolution, trained-user coverage, and renewal readiness.
