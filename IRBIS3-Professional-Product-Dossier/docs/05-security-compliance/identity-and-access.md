# Identity and Access

**Document status:** Draft baseline  
**Owner:** TBD  
**Review frequency:** Quarterly or on material change  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-24

> Govern who can use the product and data.

## Model

Use approved access request; business justification; least-privilege local rights; separate install/admin rights from use; periodic review; timely removal; group-based storage access where feasible.

## Evidence

Request, approval, entitlement assignment, device, access group, owner, start/end date, and removal confirmation.
