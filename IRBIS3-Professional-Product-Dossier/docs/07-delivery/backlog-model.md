> Define work-item hierarchy and fields.

## Hierarchy

Outcome -> Initiative -> Epic -> Feature -> Story/Task/Defect.

## Required fields

Outcome, user, problem, value, acceptance criteria, owner, target, dependencies, architecture/security/data/operations/license/cost/documentation impact, rollout and evidence.

## Initial epics

1. Establish ownership and inventory
2. Reconcile licensing and cost
3. Define supported workstation/version
4. Secure measurement data
5. Operationalize support
6. Train users
7. Decide upgrade/roadmap
